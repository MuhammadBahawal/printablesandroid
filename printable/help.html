Frequently Asked Questions

Got a specific question? Browse common topics below or contact our support team.

What is the best way to write prompts for 3D generation?

For best results, mention the subject matter, view/pose, and structural details. For example: "A medieval rogue assassin in hooded cloak, holding daggers, front T-pose, watertight miniature, clean solid base." Avoid purely abstract terms or prompts that only describe lighting; focus on shapes, clothing, weapons, and silhouettes.

Can I 3D print models generated by Printable directly?

Yes! All models created by Printable undergo automatic automated manifold geometry validation. The meshes are 100% solid, closed (watertight), and free of interior intersecting non-manifold faces. You can export them in .GLB or .OBJ, import directly into slicers like UltiMaker Cura, Bambu Studio, or PrusaSlicer, generate supports, and slice for your 3D printer.

How do touch controls work in the 360° Realtime Inspector?

When inspecting your 3D model on your Android phone:
• Single finger drag: 360° Orbit rotation around the central axis.
• Two-finger drag: Pan the camera horizontally or vertically across the mesh.
• Pinch in / out: Zoom into fine surface geometry and character textures.
• Toggle button: Switch between shaded PBR rendering and wireframe preview.

How does Guest Mode work without creating an account?

Printable values zero-friction creativity. When you open the Android app for the first time, a pseudonymous cryptographic device token is assigned to your installation. Your generation history, favorite 3D models, and credit balance are linked securely to this identifier. You do not need to create passwords or submit personal emails to generate models.

Where are downloaded 3D model files saved on Android?

When you tap Export .GLB or Export .OBJ in the app, files are saved directly into your device's public Downloads / Printable folder. You can transfer them via USB, Google Drive, SD Card, or cloud storage directly to your desktop slicing computer.

Are the 3D models I generate mine to sell commercially?

Yes. You retain commercial rights to 3D meshes generated from your original prompts, personal photographs, and sketches. You may 3D print them, cast them in resin, sell physical prints, or import them into indie video games and digital animations without paying royalties to Printable.
