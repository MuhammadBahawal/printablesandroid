Three Ways to Create. Boundless Possibilities.

Pick the input method that best fits your workflow, whether you're sketching on paper or typing an idea.

01. Text to 3D

Describe your vision in plain English. Specify character archetypes, sci-fi vehicles, mechanical props, or architectural elements, and watch the AI sculpt geometry.

02. Photo to 3D

Snap a photo of a physical figurine, an everyday object, or upload concept art from your gallery to construct an accurate 3D mesh with matched color textures.

03. Sketch to 3D

Hand-drawn line drawings on paper or in a drawing app become solid 3D geometry with calculated depth and volume contours.
