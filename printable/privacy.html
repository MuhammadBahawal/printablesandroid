Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026 • Package: com.techtornix.printable • Active Policy

At Printable, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal data. This policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and secured across the Printable AI 3D Model Generator Android application and connected web services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only limited information necessary to deliver, maintain, and optimize our 3D generation capabilities:

  • Direct User Inputs: Prompts, keywords, reference photographs, and sketch drawings provided for 3D generation.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device type, OS version, application crash logs, and performance metrics.
  • Support Communications: Names, email addresses, and message logs submitted via our support forms.

2. AI Generation Inputs and Results

When you initiate a 3D model generation request:

  • Your submitted text prompt, photo, or sketch is securely transmitted via TLS encryption to our cloud backend processing pipeline (powered by Tripo3D API infrastructure).
  • The cloud pipeline processes the input to synthesize polygon meshes, vertices, and textures.
  • Generated GLB models and preview images are returned to your device and cached in your local app storage for rapid offline access.
  • We do not sell your personal images or prompts to third-party data brokers.

3. Guest Accounts and In-App Purchases

To provide a frictionless experience, Printable utilizes anonymous Firebase Authentication, assigning a randomized guest identifier. This identifier tracks generation credit balances and project associations without demanding personal credentials.

All in-app purchases, generation tokens, and subscriptions are handled securely through the Google Play Store billing service. Printable does not collect or store full credit card numbers or payment credentials on its servers.

4. Third-Party Partners & SDKs

Our app interacts with vetted third-party service providers who comply with stringent privacy frameworks:

  • Google Play Services: Core application updates and Google Play billing processing.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud database, authentication tokens, and crash reporting diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob: Provides contextual advertising for non-pro users in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and COPPA/GDPR consent standards.
  • Tripo AI: High-performance neural processing engine for 3D geometric mesh inference.

5. Storage, Retention, and Security

We deploy robust organizational and technological safeguards. All network communications utilize modern HTTPS/TLS protocols. Local assets reside within Android application sandbox boundaries.

Support inquiries sent through our website support form are stored in our secure MongoDB database infrastructure, allowing our developers to resolve technical inquiries efficiently.

6. Your Rights and Deletion Requests

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA, LGPD), you retain the right to:

  • Access the personal information associated with your account or support messages.
  • Request permanent deletion of your stored support submissions and cloud-associated task IDs.
  • Clear local model caches and history at any time via Android System Settings > Apps > Printable > Clear Storage.
